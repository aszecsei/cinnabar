# Volumetric fog

The fog is a froxel volume: screen tiles of `FOG_TILE` (16) pixels, in the [quality](quality.md#levels)'s depth slices (`FOG_LEVEL_SLICES`: 32 to 96, and 64, two for each of the clusters' slices, at High) (`lib/engine/render/fog.odin`). Its texels are a 3D texture (see [GPU](../gpu/resources-and-pipelines.md#resources)).

- **The `Fog` settings** (stable ID 38, see [Settings](frame.md#settings)) give the frame its fog, from the volumes at the camera. `density` is the share of light that a meter of fog takes away, at and below `base_height`, a height in the world in meters. Above it, the density falls off by `height_falloff` per meter. `albedo` is the share of that light that the fog scatters, per channel (white by default). `anisotropy` is Henyey-Greenstein's g: above 0, the fog glows around a light that the camera looks toward. Without a density, or without the fog's pipelines, the frame has no fog passes.
- **Injection** (`fog_inject.hlsli`, a compute pass after the clusters and the shadow maps): each froxel takes a point in its slice, which moves through 8 places from frame to frame. It writes the scattering and the extinction there, and the light that the point scatters toward the camera: every directional light, the punctual lights of its cluster with their falloff, each with Henyey-Greenstein's phase and its shadow maps, and the ambient light from every direction. A light with a channel of the shadow mask has no shadow maps, so in a frame with a mask the traced shader (`fog_inject_traced.hlsl`) traces a ray toward it. A frame without one, because the device has no `Ray_Query` or the quality does not trace, uses `fog_inject.hlsl`. Then the froxel blends 10% of this frame (`FOG_BLEND`) into its history, read where the point was in last frame's volume. The volume is not pre-exposed.
- **Integration** (`fog_integrate.hlsl`) walks each tile's froxels from the camera outward. For each, it writes the light that the fog scatters toward the camera up to the slice's far end, and the share of light from there that reaches the camera. Each slice integrates its light as the fog dims it, so the result does not depend on the slices' thickness.
- **The forward pass** reads the volume at each pixel's position, trilinearly: `color × transmittance + scattered`, before the exposure. The view model's pixels take their place from the camera's projection, as the clusters do.
- **The background:** where nothing drew, the scene holds the `Environment`'s background, which the forward pass did not fog. A last pass (`fog_background.hlsl`) dims it by the whole volume and adds the volume's light, pre-exposed. The volume ends at the clusters' far end (`CLUSTER_FAR`, 500 m), so fog beyond it adds nothing.
- A game's own draws in `world_draw` are not fogged unless their shaders read the volume.
