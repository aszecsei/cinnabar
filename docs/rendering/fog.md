# Volumetric fog

The fog is a froxel volume: screen tiles of the [quality](quality.md#levels)'s `FOG_LEVEL_TILE` pixels, 16 at every level (see [Tile size](#tile-size)), in its depth slices (`FOG_LEVEL_SLICES`: 32 to 96, and 64, two for each of the clusters' slices, at High) (`lib/engine/render/fog.odin`). Its texels are a 3D texture (see [GPU](../gpu/resources-and-pipelines.md#resources)).

- **The `Fog` settings** (stable ID 38, see [Settings](frame.md#settings)) give the frame its fog, from the volumes at the camera. `density` is the share of light that a meter of fog takes away, at and below `base_height`, a height in the world in meters. Above it, the density falls off by `height_falloff` per meter. `albedo` is the share of that light that the fog scatters, per channel (white by default). `anisotropy` is Henyey-Greenstein's g: above 0, the fog glows around a light that the camera looks toward. Without a density, or without the fog's pipelines, the frame has no fog passes.
- **Injection** (`fog_inject.hlsli`, a compute pass after the clusters and the shadow maps): each froxel takes a point in its slice, which moves through 8 places from frame to frame. It writes the scattering and the extinction there, and the light that the point scatters toward the camera: every directional light, the punctual lights of its cluster with their falloff, each with Henyey-Greenstein's phase and its shadow maps, and the sky's light. That is the [ambient light](sky.md#ambient-light): Flat's colors, the atmosphere's, or the texture's. It goes through Henyey-Greenstein's phase as far as the ambient's first harmonics go (`ambient_scattered` in `ambient.hlsli`, `c + 1.5·g·(G·v)` with `v` from the camera toward the point; see [Ambient light](sky.md#ambient-light)). Without anisotropy it is the ambient light's average over every direction. Above 0, fog takes more of the light from the side of the sky behind it as the camera sees it, so fog toward a low sun's horizon glows with that part of the sky, and its tint follows the sky as the sun moves. The directional light that casts the [cloud shadow](clouds.md#shadow) is darkened by it through the light loop, which is the only directional shadow the fog has past the shadow maps' 100 m. A light with a channel of the shadow mask has no shadow maps, so in a frame with a mask the traced shader (`fog_inject_traced.hlsl`) traces a ray toward it, through the shadow casters of the [ray scene](ray-scene.md#ray-scene). A frame without one, because the device has no `Ray_Query` or the quality does not trace, uses `fog_inject.hlsl`. Then the froxel blends 10% of this frame (`FOG_BLEND`) into its history, read where the froxel's middle was in last frame's volume, so a still camera reads its own texel. The volume is not pre-exposed.
- **Integration** (`fog_integrate.hlsl`) walks each tile's froxels from the camera outward. For each, it writes the light that the fog scatters toward the camera up to the slice's far end, and the share of light from there that reaches the camera. Each slice integrates its light as the fog dims it, so the result does not depend on the slices' thickness.
- **In an [orthographic view](frame.md#orthographic-views)** the froxels use the clusters' even slices, each froxel is a box, and a ray through a tile is as long as its depth.
- **The forward pass** reads the volume at each pixel's position, trilinearly: `color × transmittance + scattered`, before the exposure. The view model's pixels take their place from the camera's projection, as the clusters do. In `Atmosphere`, the [aerial perspective](sky.md#aerial-perspective) comes first: the air between the camera and the pixel dims its color and adds the air's light, and the fog then dims both and adds its own (`air_and_fog_at` in `fog_at.hlsli`). The air goes on past the fog's far end, so a surface beyond 500 m has no seam there. The transparent and sorted surfaces, the sprites and the sprite renderers read both in the same way.
- **The background:** where nothing drew, the scene holds the `Environment`'s background or its [sky](sky.md#passes) with the [clouds](clouds.md#passes) composed over it, which the forward pass did not fog. A last pass (`fog_background.hlsl`), after the clouds' composite, dims it by the whole volume and adds the volume's light, pre-exposed. The volume ends at the clusters' far end (`CLUSTER_FAR`, 500 m), so fog beyond it adds nothing.
- A game's own draws in `world_draw` are not fogged unless their shaders read the volume.
- **The tile** reaches the injection and the integration in their push constants (`Fog_Push.tile`, `Integrate_Push.tile`), which place each froxel's point at its tile's center. The forward pass, the background pass and the history's lookup take the volume's share of the screen (`Frame_Constants.fog_scale`), since the last tiles reach past the screen's edges.

## Tile size

The froxels are 16 pixels across at every level. 8-pixel tiles at Ultra, as Arknights: Endfield's grid at 1080p has, were measured and left out ([#377](https://github.com/aszecsei/cinnabar/issues/377)): no capture showed a difference above the frame's own noise, and they cost about three times the injection and four times the memory.

- **Scenes:** Sponza with fog (density 0.01, anisotropy 0.6) and a sun of intensity 20, 51° up along the court, from three places looking toward the sun: down the court, whose walls cut the sunlit fog into a shaft (the court); past a pillar beside the curtains (the curtains); and up through the curtains' strips against the sunlit fog (the strips). And mygame's glass scene at its pillars (the glass). Each at Ultra, which traces the sun's shadow in the injection, in the editor's viewport at a fixed size (see [Automation](../editor/capture.md#timing)), on an RTX 5090 with D3D12.
- **Times** are the median of five profile summaries, each the average per frame over 250 ms, in microseconds:

  | View | Size | Inject, 16 px | Inject, 8 px | Integrate, 16 px | Integrate, 8 px |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Court | 1920×1080 | 72 | 229 | 8.8 | 11.6 |
  | Curtains | 1920×1080 | 61 | 195 | 8.8 | 11.3 |
  | Strips | 1920×1080 | 51 | 149 | 8.8 | 11.6 |
  | Glass | 1920×1080 | 23 | 70 | 8.8 | 11.3 |
  | Court | 2560×1440 | 90 | 321 | 12.8 | 16.3 |
  | Curtains | 2560×1440 | 78 | 284 | 12.8 | 15.5 |
  | Strips | 2560×1440 | 64 | 213 | 12.9 | 16.0 |
  | Glass | 2560×1440 | 35 | 121 | 12.0 | 16.7 |

  The injection takes 2.9 to 3.6 times as long, since it runs the light loop, the shadow maps and the traced ray at four times the froxels. The integration walks a column of froxels per thread, and four times the columns cost about a third more.
- **Memory** at Ultra's 96 slices, `RGBA16_Float`, for the history's two textures and the integrated volume: 18.8 MB at 16 pixels and 74.6 MB at 8 at 1920×1080 (120×68 and 240×135 tiles), 33.2 MB and 132.7 MB at 2560×1440.
- **The captures:** the shaft's edges in the court, the pillar's silhouette against the fog and the strips' edges against the sunlit fog look the same at both sizes, at full size and enlarged three times. Between the two sizes, `flickerstat`'s step at the 99.9th percentile at 1920×1080 is 5 codes in the court, against 4 between two captures at one size, and 3 in the glass. In the curtains it is 13 against 4, in spots on the walls, not along the fog's edges. At 2560×1440 the two captures at one size differ as much as the two sizes do.
